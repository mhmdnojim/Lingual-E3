Where on the page

While you scroll, swipe or zoom the page yourself, the page stays where you put it. It follows the reading again after the wait — or at once with Back to the reading. Tapping a sentence does not count as scrolling.

Show the translation

Prepare downloads missing translations using the internet, even when offline reading is selected. Selected text is sent to the translation service and the results are saved on this computer. Word meanings use their sentence as context.

Translation levels to save

Word-level preparation can take longer. In Word mode, hover over or click a word to show its contextual meaning. Breakdown saves what breaking a sentence down shows (the sentence, each word in it and each word on its own), so it works offline.
