LBTerms and privacy

Lingua Books makes texts clickable, read aloud and translated, for learning languages. Please read this before you add or share a book.

1. What you may add and share

  • Your books are private (only you can see them) until you choose to share one.
  • Share a book with everyone only if you have the right to: it is your own work, it is in the public domain, or its licence allows sharing (for example Creative Commons). Say which licence it has, and where it comes from.
  • Do not share books, course books or other material that someone else owns without their permission, even with a new name or cover.
  • No illegal, hateful, violent or sexual content, and nothing that harms children.
  • For your private books you are responsible for having the right to use them.

2. Problems and copyright complaints

Every public book has a Report a problem button (in the book's About tab). The admins look at every report. A book reported by several people is hidden until they have looked at it. Books that break these rules are removed, and accounts that break them again and again are closed.

If you own the rights to something shared here without your permission, report the book, or write to the site's admin, with a link to the book and what you own. It is removed quickly.

3. What is kept about you

  • Your account: e-mail address, name, and your password as a one-way hash (nobody can read it).
  • What you add: your books (the files and the pages made from them), your corrections, your MP3s.
  • One cookie: it keeps you logged in for 30 days. There are no advertising or tracking cookies. Your reading settings (voice, speed, language) stay in your own browser.
  • Reports keep the internet address of the sender for a while, against abuse.

To delete your books, use Settings → Delete. To close your account and delete everything, write to the admin.

4. Services used for some features

  • Reading aloud uses the voices of your own device. MP3s with natural voices are made with Microsoft's online voices: the sentences are sent there.
  • Translations are made with Google Translate: the sentences are sent there. Translations are machine-made and not always right.

5. No guarantee

The site is offered as it is, for learning. Texts read from photos and PDFs (OCR) can contain mistakes. We may change these terms; the date below shows the last change.
